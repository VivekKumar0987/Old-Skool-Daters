<a id="the-idea"></a>
## Why does this exist?

**Because connection should not be a premium feature.**

It should be easy to discover someone and say hello. Yet so many dating apps put obstacles in the way: swipes, likes, mutual-match gates, expensive conversation unlocks, and an inbox you have to stay inside. Something human starts feeling like a complicated transaction.

**Old Skool Daters grew out of frustration with that experience.** It is a small experiment in making the first step simple again. Open the world. Find someone. See a little of who they are. If they have chosen to share a way to be reached, say hello on that platform.

The project borrows a beautiful idea from *Akai Ito*, the Japanese **Red Thread of Fate**: people can be connected in ways we cannot plan. Here, the thread is a metaphor, **not an algorithm claiming to know your destiny**.

> **The world map makes discovery possible. The Red Thread explains why discovery matters.**

No swiping to win permission. No compatibility score deciding who matters. No payment to unlock a conversation. Just a way to discover people, everywhere, with respect for their choices and privacy.

### 🤝 Love Referrals / An introduction, not a ranking

Someone can introduce you to a person they know. The idea is a voluntary human recommendation, not a popularity contest. Permission and privacy safeguards for referrals still need work before public release.

<details>
<summary><b>How is this different from traditional matching?</b></summary>

A referral is a way to discover somebody. It is **not** a like, a match score, a paid introduction, or a promise that the other person wants to be contacted. Users still control what they share, and blocking must be respected.

</details>

## Small profiles. Clear choices.

People aren't résumés. Their public profile stays intentionally brief: **photo, unique username, availability, short description, and contact options they choose to share**.

The original idea has an intentional **Online / Offline** choice. Offline does not automatically remove someone from discovery, but an old location must never be presented as live GPS data. Approximate display and expiration rules need to be finalized before public use.

## What makes it Old Skool?

| Many dating apps | Old Skool Daters |
|:---|:---|
| Swipe until you match | Explore a real map and discover people |
| Wait for mutual-match approval | No match gate before seeing voluntarily shared contacts |
| Pay to unlock conversations | No paid messaging gate in the concept |
| Opaque compatibility ranking | No compatibility score or partner ranking |
| Stay in a built-in chat | Continue through chosen external platforms |
| Long matching questionnaires | A compact public profile |

**Less machinery between people doesn't mean fewer safety rules.** Adult eligibility, consent, blocking, reporting, protecting location privacy, and controlling public contact information matter especially in a direct-connect product.

## Run it locally

This is a **local prototype**, not an active public dating service. You can explore it on your computer without deploying anything.

**What you need:** Node.js, [pnpm](https://pnpm.io/installation), and a copy of the full application repository (including the `packages/` directory).

```bash
pnpm install
pnpm --filter shared build
pnpm db:init
pnpm dev
```

Open **http://localhost:5173**. The local service runs at **http://localhost:3001**.

<details>
<summary><b>What do these parts mean? (Plain English)</b></summary>

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **React frontend** | The screens, map and buttons people see. |
| **Express backend** | The local service that processes accounts and discovery requests. |
| **SQLite database** | A file containing the project's local account and profile records. |
| **MapLibre** | The map engine that lets people pan, zoom and open markers. |
| **PWA** | A website that can be installed like an app on supported devices. |

For routes and data fields, see [`PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md`](PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md), if included in your repository.

</details>

## Current status

**Working locally:** accounts, map exploration, username search, basic profiles, Random, Wheel of Love, BADDate, Uni Date, referral discovery, and external contact handoff, based on the developer's local reports and screenshots.

**Still to finish before public use:** location precision and stale-location safeguards, meaningful adult-only access controls, moderation, abusive-contact prevention, profile privacy, and reliable testing. The visual designs in this README are proposals, not implemented UI claims.
